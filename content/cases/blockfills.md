## Background

Creditors connected to Blockfills faced a familiar challenge: their capital was tied up while the recovery process developed, with limited certainty around timing and final distributions.

For businesses and investors, waiting on an unresolved claim can make planning difficult. The underlying value may still be meaningful, but access to that value is delayed by documentation, legal processes, and an uncertain timetable.

That uncertainty can be especially difficult for creditors who need to manage cash flow, meet obligations, or decide where to deploy capital next. A claim is an asset, but until it can be transferred or paid out, it does not provide the flexibility of cash on hand.

## Our role

VL Capital Management provided Blockfills creditors with a path to **upfront liquidity** by evaluating and acquiring eligible claims.

Our team reviewed the available claim documentation, assessed the likely recovery profile and timing, and presented clear, risk-adjusted offers. We managed the transfer process from start to finish, giving creditors a confidential way to convert a delayed position into capital they could use immediately.

The process was designed to be straightforward. Creditors could share the core information about their position, receive an initial assessment, and decide whether an offer matched their priorities. There was no requirement to proceed, and the valuation reflected the claim's documentation, expected recovery, and the time and risk involved in waiting.

By taking on the recovery timeline and related uncertainty, we gave creditors more control over the outcome they could achieve today. That allowed each claimant to weigh a known, immediate recovery against the possibility of a later distribution without having to manage the process alone.

## Outcome

- Creditors gained an immediate monetization option
- Uncertainty around timing and recovery was reduced
- Claims could be transferred through a clear, confidential process
- Capital was freed for redeployment instead of remaining tied up

The result was a practical alternative for Blockfills creditors who preferred a defined outcome and usable capital now. Rather than continuing to carry an unresolved position, eligible claimants could complete a structured transfer and move forward with greater certainty.

For creditors who valued certainty and liquidity today, selling the claim offered a practical alternative to waiting for an open-ended recovery process.
