## Background

Gatecoin entered liquidation after operations ceased, leaving many creditors with claims tied to a multi-step legal recovery process.

As with most insolvency estates, recoveries can take significant time and involve procedural uncertainty. Creditors were often forced to choose between waiting for distributions and securing certainty sooner.

## Our role

VL Capital Management provided a path to **upfront liquidity** by purchasing creditor claims at a fair, risk-adjusted valuation.

We reviewed claim documentation, assessed expected recovery timing, and structured transfers so claimants could complete the process quickly and confidentially.

## Outcome

- Creditors gained an immediate monetization option
- Exposure to timeline and recovery uncertainty was reduced
- Claims were transferred through a clear, confidential process
- Capital was unlocked for redeployment instead of remaining tied to the estate

For creditors prioritizing certainty and speed, claim monetization provided a practical alternative to a prolonged wait.
