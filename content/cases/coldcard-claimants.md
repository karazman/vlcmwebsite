## Background

Coldcard users have reported unauthorized bitcoin movements and need a structured, private way to document incidents and compare patterns.

## Our role

VL Capital Management launched an independent claimant intake for Coldcard / Coinkite-related incidents.

Our process focuses on helping victims share public transaction facts without exposing wallet secrets. We review reports for common timelines, transfer patterns, and device context, then provide coordinated updates where appropriate.

## Current status

This is an active engagement.

If you were affected, submit your report through our intake page so we can include your incident in the pattern review.
